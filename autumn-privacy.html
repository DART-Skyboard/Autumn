Autumn — Application Analytics

Autumn collects anonymous usage analytics of our own application logic for all users — including guests — across multiple sessions. This data is used solely to improve Autumn's LEATR neural network, understand how our tools are being used, and optimize performance.

What we collect (all users):

  • Tool activation patterns (e.g. which LEATR Natural Tools were invoked)
  • Session timing, frequency, and duration
  • Logic operation sequences (Arc Edge, Lead Edge Maze, Calc, Forge, etc.)
  • BRPN shell state transitions (GEO / MAR / AERO values)
  • General interaction patterns within our applications

What we do not collect from guests or unregistered users: Personal identifiers, names, contact information, or any data that could identify an individual. Guest sessions are fully anonymous.

Data sharing preferences are available in the 🔑 KEYS → Data Sharing panel inside the Autumn app. Registered users may update their preferences at any time, and opted-out data collection stops immediately upon deselection.

Registered User Data Sharing

Users who create an account may optionally elect to share personal data fields with DART Meadow for enhanced features and personalization. The following data points are individually opt-in and may be withdrawn at any time:

  • Display name — used for personalization within the app
  • Email address — for account-related communications
  • Phone number — optional, for future account recovery
  • Mailing address — optional, for physical correspondence
  • Date of birth — optional, for age-appropriate content

Each field is independently controlled. Opting out of any field immediately discontinues collection and processing of that data point. Personal data is never sold to third parties and is never included in any model training dataset without your explicit opt-in via the Model Training preference below.

Model Training Opt-In

Registered users may voluntarily opt in to allow Autumn to learn from their session memory, chat history, and application activity to help advance the LEATR v2 neural network and Autumn AI.

If you opt in:

  • Your session data is synced to a private, user-specific folder in Autumn's secure repository — inaccessible to other users
  • Data is identified only by your unique session identifier — not by your name or personal details unless you have also opted in to personal data sharing
  • If you have not opted in to personal data sharing, any personal information present in session data will be automatically omitted before sync
  • You may revoke this opt-in at any time; syncing stops immediately and existing synced data may be requested for deletion

By enabling Model Training, you confirm the data you share does not contain unlawful, malicious, or third-party confidential content, and you agree that this contribution may be used to improve Autumn AI and the LEATR system for all users.

Model Training opt-in is available in 🔑 KEYS → Data Sharing inside the Autumn app.
